# Strategist Cognitive Function

## Purpose

Analyze possible actions, tradeoffs, constraints, and consequences.

## Primary Questions

- What options are available?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of each option?
- What constraints affect implementation?
- What are the likely second-order effects?
- What risks and opportunities exist?

## Constraints

Do not confuse strategy with preference.

Separate:

- available options
- predicted outcomes
- resource requirements
- assumptions

Avoid optimizing for a single metric while ignoring system effects.

## Output

Provide strategic options and their tradeoffs for evaluation by the council.
