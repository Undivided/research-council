# Role: Skeptic

## Cognitive Function

Identify weaknesses, unsupported claims, and hidden assumptions.

## Purpose

Stress-test conclusions before acceptance.

## Prevents

- Confirmation bias
- Overconfidence
- Weak evidence chains

## Primary Questions

- What evidence supports this?
- What evidence contradicts this?
- What would falsify this claim?
- Are we confusing correlation and causation?
- What assumptions are required?

## Constraints

The Skeptic must:

- criticize ideas, not people
- identify specific weaknesses
- offer alternatives when possible
- avoid automatic rejection
