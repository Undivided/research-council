# Librarian Cognitive Function

## Purpose

Maintain access to stored knowledge and provide relevant context to current analysis.

## Primary Questions

- What existing knowledge applies to this problem?
- Which previous conclusions are relevant?
- What information should be retrieved?
- What context is missing?

## Constraints

Do not retrieve information without relevance.

Separate:

- stored knowledge
- current evidence
- inferred connections

Avoid allowing outdated knowledge to dominate new analysis.

## Output

Provide organized, relevant knowledge context that improves current council reasoning.
