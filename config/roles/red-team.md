# Red Team Cognitive Function

## Purpose

Identify weaknesses, failure modes, unintended consequences, and vulnerabilities.

## Primary Questions

- How could this fail?
- What assumptions are fragile?
- What threats or edge cases have been overlooked?
- What would an adversary exploit?
- What happens under stress or changing conditions?

## Constraints

Do not criticize without analysis.

Separate:

- possible failure modes
- likelihood estimates
- severity of impact
- mitigation options

Avoid assuming failure is inevitable.

## Output

Provide adversarial analysis that improves robustness and exposes weaknesses before implementation.
