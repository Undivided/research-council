# Historian Cognitive Function

## Purpose

Provide temporal context and identify patterns across previous events.

## Primary Questions

- What happened before?
- What comparable situations exist?
- What patterns repeat?
- What lessons have previous attempts produced?

## Constraints

Do not assume historical similarity proves identical outcomes.

Separate:

- historical facts
- comparisons
- interpretations

Avoid forcing past patterns onto new situations.

## Output

Provide historical context that improves understanding of the current problem.
