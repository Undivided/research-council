# Research Questions

## Philosophy

- What is truth?
- How do humans know something is true?
- What are the limits of human knowledge?

---

## History

- How have societies handled propaganda before?
- What examples exist of widespread false beliefs?
- How did people eventually correct errors?

---

## Psychology

- Why are humans vulnerable to misinformation?
- How do identity and emotion affect reasoning?
- What causes cognitive dissonance?

---

## Strategy

- How do states, organizations, and groups use information?
- What incentives create misleading narratives?
- How does information become a weapon?

---

## Technology

- How has the internet changed information?
- How does AI alter the information environment?
- Does increased information improve understanding?

---

## Practical Application

- How should an individual evaluate claims?
- What habits produce better judgment?
- How should uncertainty be managed?
