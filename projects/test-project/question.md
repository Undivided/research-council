# World Assessment 2026

## Primary Question

What is the most accurate possible understanding of the current world situation?

## Goals

- Separate facts from narratives.
- Identify reliable information sources.
- Understand historical context.
- Identify incentives and information warfare.
- Test conclusions against opposing viewpoints.

## Constraints

- Avoid ideological assumptions.
- Do not confuse popularity with truth.
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
