# Mission 001

## Title

Understanding Truth-Seeking in the Information Age

---

## Core Question

How can a person or society build an accurate understanding of reality when information is affected by propaganda, incentives, psychological bias, and competing narratives?

---

## Objective

Develop a framework for evaluating information.

The council must investigate:

- How humans determine truth.
- How propaganda works historically.
- How modern information systems affect perception.
- How incentives shape narratives.
- How to distinguish disagreement from deception.

---

## Constraints

The council must avoid:

- Assuming one side has all truth.
- Treating disagreement as proof of bad faith.
- Confusing confidence with evidence.
- Using emotional impact as evidence.

---

## Desired Outcome

Create a practical method for answering:

"What should I believe, and why?"
