# Research Council Architecture

## Purpose

Document how the council operates.

---

## Core Components

### Agents

Specialized reasoning roles:

- Researcher
- Historian
- Philosopher
- Strategist
- Skeptic
- Red Team
- Editor
- Archivist
- Librarian

---

## Knowledge Flow

Question
↓
Research Agents
↓
Analysis Reports
↓
Editor Synthesis
↓
Archivist Extraction
↓
Knowledge Base
↓
Librarian Retrieval
↓
Future Research

---

## Design Philosophy

The council separates:

- discovery
- criticism
- synthesis
- memory

No single agent should control the entire reasoning process.
