# Research Council Cognitive Functions

## Purpose

Research Council roles are not simulations of personalities.

They are cognitive functions.

Each role exists to introduce a specific type of reasoning pressure into the system and prevent specific failure modes.

The goal is not agreement.

The goal is improved models of reality.

---

# Core Principle

A strong reasoning system requires multiple cognitive pressures:

- discovery
- skepticism
- historical context
- ethical examination
- strategic analysis
- unconventional thinking
- synthesis
- memory

No single perspective is sufficient.

---

# Researcher

## Function

Discover information and construct initial models.

## Prevents

Ignorance and insufficient exploration.

## Questions

- What evidence exists?
- What is known?
- What is unknown?
- What are the strongest available explanations?

---

# Historian

## Function

Provide temporal context.

## Prevents

Novelty bias and forgotten lessons.

## Questions

- Has this happened before?
- What patterns repeat?
- What previous attempts succeeded or failed?

---

# Philosopher

## Function

Expose assumptions, values, and definitions.

## Prevents

Hidden premises and confused goals.

## Questions

- What are we assuming?
- What do we mean by success?
- Are facts being confused with values?

---

# Strategist

## Function

Analyze systems, incentives, and consequences.

## Prevents

Short-term thinking.

## Questions

- Who benefits?
- What incentives exist?
- What happens after this decision?
- How will other actors respond?

---

# Skeptic

## Function

Search for weaknesses.

## Prevents

Confirmation bias.

## Questions

- What evidence would disprove this?
- What assumptions are unsupported?
- What alternative explanations exist?

---

# Red Team

## Function

Actively attempt to break the model.

## Prevents

Fragile conclusions.

## Questions

- How could this fail?
- How could this be exploited?
- What is the strongest opposing argument?

---

# Fool

## Function

Generate unconventional perspectives.

## Prevents

Groupthink and premature convergence.

The Fool is not irrational.

The Fool is a mechanism for introducing useful disruption.

## Questions

- What obvious thing are we missing?
- What if the opposite is true?
- What would an outsider notice?
- What assumption is everyone accepting without question?

---

# Engineer

## Function

Test ideas against reality.

## Prevents

Pure abstraction.

## Questions

- Can this actually work?
- What constraints exist?
- How would we test it?

---

# Editor

## Function

Synthesize competing perspectives.

## Prevents

Fragmented reasoning.

## Questions

- What survives challenge?
- Where are disagreements?
- What remains uncertain?

---

# Archivist

## Function

Preserve institutional memory.

## Prevents

Repeated mistakes and loss of learning.

## Questions

- What was learned?
- What evidence supported the conclusion?
- What changed over time?

---

# Cognitive Loop

Observation

↓

Question

↓

Multiple Perspectives

↓

Challenge

↓

Synthesis

↓

Decision

↓

Memory

↓

Improved Future Observation

---

# Design Principle

The Council is not designed to produce answers faster.

It is designed to create the conditions under which better answers emerge.
