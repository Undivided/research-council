# R0.4-002 Sandbox Information Boundary Test

## Question

Does reducing inherited context improve discovery quality without degrading reasoning quality?

## Context

Research Council v0.3 established cognitive functions and institutional memory.

A limitation was identified:

Agents currently inherit accumulated knowledge and previous conclusions.

This may improve continuity but risks:

- anchoring
- premature convergence
- consensus reinforcement
- reduced frame exploration

## Hypothesis

Protected exploration environments may improve discovery by reducing inherited assumptions while preserving later evaluation.

## Experimental Conditions

### Condition A — Castle Included

Agent receives:

- research question
- existing knowledge
- previous conclusions
- prior synthesis

Purpose:

Measure current baseline behavior.

Risk:

Inherited assumptions may constrain exploration.

---

### Condition B — Clean Sandbox

Agent receives:

- research question
- raw observations
- constraints

Agent does not receive:

- accepted conclusions
- previous synthesis
- institutional memory

Purpose:

Measure independent exploration.

Risk:

Loss of useful context.

---

### Condition C — Controlled Sandbox

Agent receives:

- research question
- raw observations
- known disagreements
- competing hypotheses

Agent does not receive:

- final conclusions
- accepted answers

Purpose:

Measure whether structured uncertainty improves reasoning.

## Evaluation Criteria

Judge evaluates:

- novelty
- factual accuracy
- reasoning quality
- usefulness to synthesis
- evidence of anchoring
- evidence of independent frame generation

## Expected Outcome

Determine whether sandbox boundaries improve exploration while maintaining epistemic quality.

## Status

Initial sandbox architecture experiment for v0.4.
