# R0.4-002 Observation 001

## Experiment

Sandbox Information Boundary Test

## Condition Tested

Fool agent operating without inherited institutional context.

Removed:

- previous council knowledge
- knowledge base
- current council reports

Preserved:

- research question
- Fool cognitive function definition

## Result

The sandboxed Fool produced different reasoning behavior compared with prior integrated-context runs.

## Observed Effects

Positive:

- challenged the structure of the question itself
- identified hidden assumptions
- generated alternative conceptual frames
- avoided simply repeating archived conclusions

Examples:

- questioned whether Earth vs. Mars is a false dichotomy
- questioned whether "permanent settlements" is the correct future model
- challenged whether biological humans should be the assumed unit of expansion

## Unknowns

The experiment does not yet establish:

- whether generated ideas are more accurate
- whether novelty improves final synthesis
- whether reduced context creates useful discovery or additional noise

## Preliminary Interpretation

Removing inherited context appears to increase frame exploration.

Further evaluation is required through Editor and Judge review.

## Status

Observation recorded. Awaiting adjudication.
