# R0.5-001 Observation 007

## Finding

The final institutional-memory success criterion is not demonstrated by the
existing v0.5 specimen.

The Editor explicitly distinguished sandboxed Fool material from other Council
material.

In the Editor synthesis, the following section was present:

    Exploratory/Sandboxed Material (Fool Agent)

The explicitly sandboxed hypotheses included:

- The Symbiotic Loop
- Non-Physical Settlement
- The "Zoo" Hypothesis

None of these three hypotheses appears in the downstream Archivist report or
the archived research-entry specimen.

## Downstream Trace

The Archivist report and archived research-entry specimen do preserve neighboring
strategic material from the Editor synthesis, including items such as:

    The Controlled Environment Paradox
    The Psychological Catalyst
    The Biological Definition Shift

However, the explicitly sandboxed Fool hypotheses are absent.

The Archivist report also contains no explicit language indicating that the
sandboxed material was:

    rejected
    excluded
    intentionally omitted
    retained as exploratory
    withheld from promotion

Therefore the absence of the exploratory material cannot itself demonstrate
that the Archivist recognized its epistemic status and deliberately treated it
differently.

## Interpretation

The Editor successfully performs an exploratory-versus-non-exploratory
distinction.

That distinction does not survive into the Archivist report.

The current runtime constructs persistent knowledge by extracting the
Archivist's ARCHIVAL REPORT. Therefore material omitted by the Archivist
cannot subsequently regain its exploratory classification at the
knowledge-writing step.

Once the sandboxed material disappears from the Archivist report, the
institution cannot determine whether it was:

    intentionally rejected because of provenance
    filtered by Archivist judgment
    lost during summarization
    omitted accidentally
    ignored for unrelated reasons

Silence is therefore insufficient evidence of epistemic disposition.

## Success Criterion

The remaining R0.5-001 success criterion is:

    institutional memory can distinguish exploratory from accepted material

Current result:

    NOT DEMONSTRATED

R0.5-001 therefore cannot receive PASS under its original success criteria in
the current implementation.

This finding does not demonstrate that first-class provenance is ineffective.

It demonstrates that the current durable-memory boundary does not preserve
enough information to show what happened to exploratory material.

## Architectural Gap

A durable cognitive institution may need to preserve not only admitted
knowledge, but also the disposition of significant material that was considered
and not promoted.

Conceptually:

    candidate material
    -> epistemic classification
    -> adjudication
    -> disposition
    -> durable memory

Possible dispositions may eventually include states such as:

    accepted
    exploratory
    rejected
    deferred
    unresolved

These states are not implemented by this observation and should not be treated
as a new provenance framework without further experiment.

## Architectural Principle

Absence from memory is not evidence of rejection.

If epistemic disposition matters, the institution must preserve enough evidence
to distinguish deliberate non-admission from information loss.
