# Council Protocol

## Rule 1
No single agent determines truth.

## Rule 2
Evidence quality matters more than emotional impact.

## Rule 3
Disagreement is expected.

## Rule 4
The skeptic must always attempt falsification.

## Rule 5
The final report must show uncertainty.

---

## Decision Process

Researcher:
"What evidence exists?"

Historian:
"What patterns exist?"

Strategist:
"What incentives exist?"

Philosopher:
"What assumptions exist?"

Skeptic:
"What could be wrong?"

Editor:
"What can we responsibly conclude?"
